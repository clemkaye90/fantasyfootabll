# QB Injury Report (as of 2026-09-03)

This report is time-sensitive. Treat any player not listed here as having no known injury concern as of the date above, and note that status can change day to day during camp and week to week during the season.

**Dillon Gabriel** (CLE, QB) — Status: Placed on IR. Injury: Back. Fantasy impact: It is unclear when Gabriel suffered the injury. He will miss at least the first four weeks during the regular season.. (Updated 2026-08-30)

**Patrick Mahomes II** (KC, QB) — Status: On track to start Week 1. Injury: Knee. Fantasy impact: Mahomes has worked his way back from a torn ACL. He didn't see any action in the preseason, but he is expected to be good to go for the season opener.. (Updated 2026-08-29)

**Michael Penix Jr.** (ATL, QB) — Status: Cleared for team drills. Injury: Knee. Fantasy impact: Penix has officially cleared all hurdles on his way back to full health. It remains unclear who will start in Week 1 between him and Tua Tagovailoa. This will remain a situation to monitor over the next couple of weeks.. (Updated 2026-08-22)

**Carson Beck** (ARI, QB) — Status: Unlikely to play Saturday. Injury: Ribs. Fantasy impact: Beck recently suffered an injury to his ribs. It's not considered a serious issue at this point, but it still looks like the team will have him sit out on Saturday as a precautionary measure.. (Updated 2026-08-20)

**Justin Fields** (KC, QB) — Status: Likely to start Saturday. Injury: None listed. Fantasy impact: Fields is expected to be Kansas City's starter again on Saturday in their preseason matchup against the Buccaneers. He isn't expected to be the team's starter heading into the regular season, however, unless there is a setback to the recovery of Patrick Mahomes II.. (Updated 2026-08-20)

**Mitchell Trubisky** (TEN, QB) — Status: Returns to practice. Injury: Bicep. Fantasy impact: Trubisky was being evaluated for a bicep injury to open the week. He is competing for the backup role.. (Updated 2026-08-19)

**Haynes King** (CAR, QB) — Status: Will not play this week. Injury: Hamstring. Fantasy impact: King is continuing to work his way back from a hamstring injury he suffered in the Hall of Fame game.. (Updated 2026-08-19)
