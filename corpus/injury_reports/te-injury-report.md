# TE Injury Report (as of 2026-09-03)

This report is time-sensitive. Treat any player not listed here as having no known injury concern as of the date above, and note that status can change day to day during camp and week to week during the season.

**Grant Calcaterra** (PHI, TE) — Status: Placed on IR. Injury: Unspecified. Fantasy impact: Calcaterra will miss at least the first four weeks of the season. It is unclear what injury he is dealing with.. (Updated 2026-09-01)

**Tip Reiman** (ARI, TE) — Status: Reserve/PUP list. Injury: Ankle. Fantasy impact: Reiman will miss at least the first four weeks of the season. He'll add depth in the tight end room when he is healthy.. (Updated 2026-09-01)

**Theo Johnson** (NYG, TE) — Status: Non-contact jersey practice. Injury: Shoulder. Fantasy impact: Johnson will have the next week and a half to get fully healthy for the start of the season.. (Updated 2026-09-01)

**Luke Musgrave** (GB, TE) — Status: Expected to play sometime in 2026. Injury: Neck. Fantasy impact: Musgrave does not hold a clear timetable to return as he works his way back from a neck injury. Jonnu Smith will backup Tucker Kraft to begin the season.. (Updated 2026-09-01)

**Tyler Warren** (IND, TE) — Status: Practicing. Injury: Groin. Fantasy impact: Warren has been working his way back from a minor groin issue. He should be good to go for Week 1 next week.. (Updated 2026-09-01)

**Tucker Kraft** (GB, TE) — Status: Participating in team drills. Injury: Knee. Fantasy impact: Kraft returned to team drills earlier in August. He remains on track to be good to go for Week 1 in September.. (Updated 2026-08-26)

**Sam LaPorta** (DET, TE) — Status: Returned to practice. Injury: Hip. Fantasy impact: Laporta's hip injury has made his status for Week 1 murky, but his returning to practice this early is a good sign. The tight end will likely be limited as he builds back up, but he could be in line to start the season barring any setbacks.. (Updated 2026-08-25)

**Ben Sinnott** (WAS, TE) — Status: Dealing with oblique injury. Injury: Oblique. Fantasy impact: The third year tight end is primarily a depth option at this point with little fantasy value. That's not to say he hasn't shown potential, he just needs to be given the role. It doesn't seem like that's coming anytime soon though.. (Updated 2026-08-25)

**George Kittle** (SF, TE) — Status: Activated from PUP. Injury: Achilles. Fantasy impact: Kittle was trending towards playing in Week 1, and this is a big step in that direction. He could be stepping into a big target share, especially with Mike Evans and De'Zhaun Stribling dealing with injuries of their own. Granted, the receivers injuries don't sound serious, but they're still noteworthy.. (Updated 2026-08-23)
