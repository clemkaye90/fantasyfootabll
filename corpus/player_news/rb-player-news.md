# RB Player News (as of 2026-08-20)

This is a snapshot of recent news, filtered to this app's own top-ranked players at this position. It is highly time-sensitive -- treat anything not listed here as having no recent news as of the date above, not as a sign nothing has happened.

**Quinshon Judkins** (CLE, RB21) — Quinshon Judkins not seen at practice Thursday. Missed Wednesday practice due to a 'minor issue' and was absent again Thursday. He remains day-to-day. (Reported 2026-08-20)

**Rachaad White** (WAS, RB43) — Rachaad White (hamstring) won't play Saturday. Will sit out Saturday's preseason game against Detroit due to a minor hamstring injury, though HC Dan Quinn says he is 'certainly close' to returning. Jacory Croskey-Merritt could benefit from an extended absence. (Reported 2026-08-20)

**Tony Pollard** (TEN, RB32) — Tony Pollard (foot) returns to practice Wednesday. Returned to practice after missing two sessions with a foot injury. Saw four carries in the preseason opener. (Reported 2026-08-19)

**Alvin Kamara** (NO, RB45) — Alvin Kamara (knee) sidelined a month with sprained MCL. Expected to miss approximately one month after a sprained MCL. Expect a slow start and a ramp-up period once he returns; Devin Neal and Kendre Miller will fight for the primary backup spot behind Travis Etienne. (Reported 2026-08-19)

**Jadarian Price** (SEA, RB25) — Jadarian Price (leg) returns to practice. Returned to full practice participation after a leg injury. Should remain on track for the season opener, though immediate role is unclear. (Reported 2026-08-18)

**Christian McCaffrey** (SF, RB3) — Christian McCaffrey would have practiced if it wasn't joint practice. 49ers HC Kyle Shanahan clarified McCaffrey would have practiced Tuesday if not for the joint-practice format, denying contract-related speculation. Remains day-to-day. (Reported 2026-08-18)

**Jeremiyah Love** (ARI, RB14) — Jeremiyah Love will not require surgery on ankle injury. Cardinals HC Mike LaFleur confirmed Love won't need surgery for his high ankle sprain and remains hopeful for the season opener. (Reported 2026-08-18)

**Kyle Monangai** (CHI, RB37) — Kyle Monangai (knee) considered week-to-week. Bears coach Ben Johnson classified the hyperextended knee injury as week-to-week; status needs continued monitoring heading into Week 1. (Reported 2026-08-18)

**Breece Hall** (NYJ, RB13) — Breece Hall (groin) out at least two weeks. Injured his groin Monday and will miss the next couple of weeks. HC Aaron Glenn expects him ready for Week 1 despite the setback. (Reported 2026-08-18)

**Josh Jacobs** (GB, RB11) — Josh Jacobs (groin) to return to practice Tuesday. Will return to practice after missing recent sessions due to a groin injury. If healthy, should maintain the RB1 workload. (Reported 2026-08-18)
