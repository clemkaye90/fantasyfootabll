# WR Player News (as of 2026-09-03)

This is a snapshot of recent news, filtered to this app's own top-ranked players at this position. It is highly time-sensitive -- treat anything not listed here as having no recent news as of the date above, not as a sign nothing has happened.

**Rome Odunze** (CHI, WR23) — Rome Odunze leaves practice early Thursday. Odunze came up hobbling during an install period at the start of practice. He tried to return to practice, but trainers wouldn't let him. This will remain something to monitor into next week. (Reported 2026-09-03)

**Alec Pierce** (IND, WR27) — Alec Pierce (ankle) practices in full on Thursday. Pierce practiced in full for the first time this year after a lengthy time off the field while recovering from ankle injuries. He will operate as the Colts' primary deep threat this season when on the field, opening things up for Josh Downs and Tyler Warren over the middle and short areas. (Reported 2026-09-03)

**Michael Wilson** (ARI, WR40) — Michael Wilson agrees to three year, $75 million deal with Cardinals. Wilson was about to be playing on the final year of his rookie contract, but will now see himself attached to the team for the long haul. It will be interesting to see how his role changes, if at all, under a new coaching staff and with Marvin Harrison Jr. back in the fold. (Reported 2026-09-03)
